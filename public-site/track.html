The Track

Our pinewood track has four lanes and is 32 feet long. We use an electronic starting gate and timer to record each car's race time. Because of variations in wood grain and construction, some lanes will be consistently faster or slower than others. Because of this, we format the event so that each car will race once on each lane. Cars are placed based on the best time on all four lanes.
